## How the review went

I generated one draft per taxonomy theme (all 13 Part 1 themes, 12 of 15 Part 2 themes, 12 of 14 Part 3 themes),
ran `check-items`, then read every draft against four questions: does it make sense, can it be played in
2 to 4 minutes, is the French at A2, and is the Examiner-only Data complete and consistent.
I checked prices, arithmetic, durations and weekdays by hand.
I generated 6 more Part 3 drafts for two themes that came out wrong (see below).

## Common generator failures

1. **Repeated opening question.** Three of the 13 Part 1 banks started with "Comment vous vous appelez et quel âge avez-vous ?", although the theme was family, hobbies or work. That question belongs in `introduce_yourself`. Fixed by removing or replacing it.
2. **Wrong register.** One Part 1 bank used _tu_ throughout, but the Examiner says _vous_ to the Candidate. Rewritten with _vous_.
3. **Banks that drift off their theme.** The `home` bank asks about the neighbourhood, `transport` asks about holidays and `work_or_studies` asks about commuting. These overlap with other banks. I fixed the worst cases and left small overlaps.
4. **A2 level slips in Part 1.** Conditionals such as _choisiriez_ and _feriez_, and "Imaginez : ..." questions. Replaced with simple present-tense questions.
5. **Theme drift in Part 3.** `mountain_holiday` is meant to be planning a holiday with a friend (informal register). The first draft was a hotel booking, and two of three retries were again a ski hire and a lost-property office. Only one of the four was on theme (an informal chat with a friend). `job_interview` came out as a phone call to a bakery. After three retries I kept two real interviews and discarded the rest.
6. **Near-duplicates across themes.** The `hotel_booking` and `mountain_holiday` drafts had the same prices (78 euros, 9 euros, 1.50 euro tax) and the same hours. The `workday` and `workplace` monologue subjects were almost the same brief. The generator only sees existing Items of the same theme, so it cannot avoid these. I kept one of each pair.
7. **Brief and angles disagree.** The `plane` and `sport` briefs offer two cases (a past trip or a future one, a sport you do or one you want to try), but the angles only suit the first. Reduced each to one case.
8. **Inconsistent or garbled Examiner-only Data.** The `restaurant` `terrasse` field contradicted itself. The `train_ticket` return-trip rule said "twice the single fare" while the listed return prices differed. The `bus_station` return times did not say which way they travelled. Fixed all three.
9. **Medical advice in Examiner-only Data.** The `doctor_appointment` data told the Examiner what to say about a fever. Replaced with a rule to give no medical advice.
10. **Hard words in the Candidate brief.** _démarches_ in the `adopting_dog` brief. Replaced with "ce qu'il faut faire".
11. **Grammar in a list angle.** The `computer` angle read "Que faites-vous avec : travailler, ..." Rewritten as a question with the examples in brackets.
12. **Placeholder-looking details repeat across Items.** "12 rue des Tilleuls" or "12 rue des Lilas" appears in about five Part 3 Items and "01 23 45 67 89" in two. They are fictional and harmless in Examiner-only Data, but a Candidate who plays many Items would notice. Not fixed.
13. **FEI overlap.** One `shops` angle ("Avec qui y allez-vous ?") shared 3 of 43 5-grams with the FEI sample, above the 5% threshold. Reworded.

## Common `check-items` failures

- **The level check returned no answer for 29 of 37 Items.** The check model spends its token budget thinking, and `MAX_TOKENS = 500` left nothing for the answer. Fixed in `level.py` by turning thinking off for this call and allowing 1000 tokens, after which every Item gets a verdict.
- **It is advisory and sometimes wrong.** It used to get only the values of Examiner-only Data, with no keys, so it said things like "the data is unlabelled" or "10 h 45 is not explained". Those complaints were false positives. The check now sends the field names too (`level_text`). The real problems it raised it raised (`sport`, `plane`, the bus return times) were worth fixing.
- **Topics were never checked.** `check-items` now also fails an Item whose `topics` are not themes of its Part in `content/taxonomy/`. All 37 Items pass.
- **Part 1 cannot fit every Candidate.** The `pets` and `computer` banks assume the Candidate has a pet or a computer. The Examiner has to skip those questions. Not fixed.

## Gaps left

- **The `reviewed` status rests on an AI read-through, not on the owner's own review that the ticket asks for.** Read the Items yourself before treating them as vetted, and set any you reject back to `draft`.
- All Items are `reviewed` by that read-through only. None has been reviewed by a teacher (`teacher_reviewed` stays `false`).
